Migration applies ONLY to 0.5.1 or older. From 0.5.2 no migration.

C64 for Analogue Pocket: new identity toby01019.C64

Platform ID: c64 (Commodore 64). The MiSTer basis is unchanged. This rename
does not change the FPGA bitstream or game data.

Existing installation:
1. Power off Pocket and back up the entire SD card first.
2. Extract the new SD ZIP to the card root and merge folders.
3. COPY your ROMs, games and V027-AUDIO.REU from Assets/c64mister/common/
   to Assets/c64/common/. Do not blindly overwrite same-named files:
   other C64 cores share this asset directory.
4. Launch toby01019.C64. Reapply your preferred core settings and reselect
   disks/cartridges. Automatic migration of old settings or saved file paths
   is not guaranteed. Your backup retains the original settings.
5. After a successful test, move Cores/Experimental.C64MiSTer/ to your backup
   to avoid a duplicate core. Retain old Assets and Settings as fallback.

Fresh installation: supply basic.bin, characters.bin, kernal.bin and
1541.bin in Assets/c64/common/. System ROMs are not included.
Do not modify other installed C64 core directories. The shared c64 platform
does not represent new hardware and does not replace other C64 cores.
