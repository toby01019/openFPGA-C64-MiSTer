0.5.3-alpha hardware test status (27 October 2026)
Independent tester confirmed all three requested checks with D64 and G64:
automatic LOAD/game start, manual LOAD*, and in-game disk swap without
unwanted LOAD/RUN. Public release uses the same tested FPGA bitstream.
Earlier reports cover Dock USB keyboard, picture/audio and other functions;
they are not claimed as a fresh exhaustive regression test for this release.
No installation through pupdate or Pocket Sync was performed for 0.5.3.
